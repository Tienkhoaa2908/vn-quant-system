# V75 accelerated research batching policy

Effective after the V74 workstation coverage failure.

1. Do not allocate a workstation cycle to a single collector probe, single threshold, or single explanatory metric if independent lanes can be executed safely in one package.
2. External-data lanes (macro, official metadata) must be isolated: fail closed for their own conclusions but may not block local-data C3/factor research unless they are primary truth inputs.
3. Each one-shot package should answer several predeclared questions in parallel while preserving causal boundaries and multiple-testing correction.
4. Every candidate must reach the same deep-backtest layer in the same package; predictive metrics alone do not earn another workstation cycle.
5. 2026/August are observed shadow data and may explain failures but may not define thresholds, windows, blend fractions or selection gates.
6. After a consolidated package fails to find a robust historical enhancement, stop expanding historical search space and move to fresh paper holdout/data-lineage completion rather than continuing threshold mining.
